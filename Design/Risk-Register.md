**One risk is added, R13**, and it is the review's finding rather than a tidy-up. Almost every
slice in the plan reports a mutation tally, and those tallies are the strongest evidence the port
has that a green suite means something. `AGENTS.md` §6 records the METHOD in full; nothing records
WHICH mutants were run. So no tally in this corpus can be re-checked — not by a reviewer, not by a
later session, not by the person who wrote it — and §6.119 is the demonstration that a tally can be
confidently wrong. The register had no row for evidence that cannot be reproduced, and it needs
one. The other categories held: three days of building found defects the register predicted.
