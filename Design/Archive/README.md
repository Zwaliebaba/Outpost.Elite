# Design/Archive/ — superseded designs, kept whole

A design comes here when a later document replaces it as the plan for its area. It is moved rather
than cut, because the useful part of a superseded plan is its record: what was measured, what was
built from it and what a ruling declined, none of which a replacement should have to restate. The
replacement says, in its own §1, what was still live on the day of the move and where it went.

Nothing in this folder is maintained. Numbers here are history and carry no `count` markers; code
comments and journal entries elsewhere still cite these files by their old names, and the slice
labels they cite — `I-1`, `T-0`, `RN-0` — resolve here unchanged.
